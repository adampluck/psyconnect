Sunday 1 st November: All Souls Day Concert – ‘The Power of Music²’ We are delighted to present ‘The Power of Music²’ on Sunday, 1 st November at 6.30 in Colet House’s beautiful Top Studio.  This will be an inspiring and uplifting day exploring the physical and emotional benefits of music in our lives: 5:00-6:00 pm - Pre-formance Talk by Dr Laszlo Nagy, with Dr Mark Ross Clark and Dr Chika Robertson (East Studio) 6:15 pm – Doors open (Top Studio) 6.30 pm – Concert Performance (Top Studio) 7.30 pm – Reception This special event is sponsored by the Music Mind Spirit Trust (MMST) , a charity that will be featuring talented musicians from two of UK’s leading music conservatoires – gifted young instrumentalists from the Royal Academy of Music , with selected singers from the Royal College of Music in London .  MMST has been in existence for more than 20 years, and produces unique events that promote the medical and well-being benefits of singing music of all styles. By popular demand, we are able to welcome back the engaging tenor and paediatric neuro-surgeon Dr Laszlo Nagy , who stands as a symbol of the love of singing with a love of the values of medicine.  Laszlo returns to Colet House with MMST’s Young Artist Musical Ambassadors and the British Opera Academy for this memorable event. There will also be the opportunity for audience members to sing a special choir piece in the newly formed Colet Chorus if they wish! (There will be a run-through rehearsal with Dr Mark Ross Clark offered on Saturday 24 Oct from 5.15-6 pm, following the Teatime Concert, for those who can attend. There will also be rehearsal opportunities from 4.30-5 pm and 6 -6.15 pm on 1 st November prior to the concert.)  Interested singers should contact the office or email Chika Robertson for further details. mailto:musicmindspirit@gmail.com We hope you can join us for our joyous Ancestry Appreciation Celebration on All Souls Day.  Experience your own powerful musical memories through intergenerational song-sharing as we pay tribute to our families and communities through this valuable mentorship opportunity. Ticket price includes the Pre-formance Talk, Concert, soft drinks and refreshments at the Reception.  (Wine will be available for a small donation).  All proceeds and donations received will support future musical scholarships and performances for the young musicians. Tickets: Adults £15 (seniors £12) Children / students £5
