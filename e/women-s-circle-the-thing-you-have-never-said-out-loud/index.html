Women's Circle: The Thing You Have Never Said Out Loud

Say the thing you've never said out loud in a judgment-free circle with other women.

Good girls don't. At your age? That's dirty. You're too much. Most of us carry a voice with an opinion about what we want. It sounds like ours, but it was learned: a mother at the front door, a school playground, a partner who laughed at the wrong moment. And very often other women, and the looks we learned to give each other. This is a morning for a small group of women to find out they are not the only one, put that judgement down, and say out loud what they actually want. Fully clothed. Nobody touches anybody. Nobody is asked to explain themselves, and you can pass on anything. Sunday 11 October, 11:00 to 13:30 (doors 10:45), North London N11. The exact address is sent on booking. Small group, often three or four women and never more than eight. Herbal teas and water are available throughout. Concession £15 | Standard £20 | Supporter £25.
