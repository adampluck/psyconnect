Soma & Eros Connection Lab Picture this: a playful Saturday afternoon, a room full of curious people, and space to practice consent and boundaries — not as a concept, but as something you feel, live, in real time. It's social, it's warm and it's a lovely place to meet people while practicing what you've learned with us, or picked up elsewhere, around saying (and hearing) yes, no, and everything in between. Beyond yes, no, maybe Consent isn't just a mechanical checklist. It's so much richer than that. We'll go beyond the body-only dimension and lean into the messier, more human stuff underneath — emotional needs, relational patterns, the things we want but rarely say out loud. What to expect Movement and contact dance Invitations into your primal body and emotional self The beautiful, strange dance of what happens when two people meet — the pull, the merging, the unique constellation that forms between two people in connection Conscious, consensual touch, held gently and at your own pace Nothing is mandatory. Everything unfolds as an invitation. You follow what feels right and leave the rest. Who this is for New to the scene? Perfect. This is exactly the kind of gentle, welcoming space to dip a toe into the world of authentic relating for the first time. Been around a while? Also perfect.
