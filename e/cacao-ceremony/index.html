Cacao Ceremony

Ceremonial cacao meets Kundalini yoga and shamanic meditation with facilitator Consuelo Cogrossi.

Rooted in one of the world’s oldest holistic traditions, this ceremony blends ceremonial-grade Cacao with Kundalini yoga, breathwork, and guided shamanic meditation. Cacao has been used as a sacred plant medicine for centuries. Today, we bring these ancient rituals into the modern world, helping you slow down, soften, and welcome more ease. You’ll experience a gentle yet powerful shift, from stress to stillness, from mind to heart. This safe space invites deep reflection, emotional release, and mind-body connection, leaving you grounded, open, and aligned. What to expect: 18:50–19:00 – Arrival & Welcome 19:00–19:30 – Kundalini Yoga 19:30–19:50 – Cacao Ritual 19:50–20:00 – Breathwork 20:00–20:20 – Guided Shamanic Meditation 20:20–20:30 – Integration & Farewell What to bring: Wear comfortable clothes and bring layers. Also bring a journal and a pen. Do I need any experience? Not at all. You don’t need to be flexible, spiritual, or familiar with cacao. Just bring your curiosity and an open mind. I’ll guide you through each practice in a safe, supportive way. What’s Cacao? A plant-medicine drink made from pure cacao beans and water, no refined sugar. Earthy, warm, and gently bitter, it nourishes the body with natural minerals and invites the mind to soften. Come as you are, straight from a busy day, or with a heart full of curiosity. Leave with ease, slowness, and a renewed sense of possibility. Book your spot today! Host : Consuelo Cogrossi, https://www.houseofsomatics.com/about
