MEN’S CIRCLE
Lone Fought Battles

What is the battle you are fighting that no one knows about?

There are battles we fight that nobody sees, the ones that don't leave a mark on the outside.

The fear you haven't told anyone about.
The decision you keep turning over in your head.
The relationship you're struggling to understand.
The loss you're still carrying.
The habit you can't seem to break.
The pressure you feel to hold everything together.
The part of yourself you're quietly questioning.

You go to work.
You show up for people.
You get on with things.

And yet, somewhere beneath all of that, there may be a battle taking place. One that you have become very good at fighting alone.

Maybe because you don't want to burden anyone.
Perhaps because you don't know how to talk about it.
Or because you learned that being a man meant handling things yourself.
Or perhaps you've simply forgotten what it feels like to let someone stand beside you when things are difficult.

So you keep it to yourself. You think about it. You manage it. You distract yourself from it. You tell yourself you'll deal with it when things calm down.

But some battles don't disappear because we become better at carrying them.

Sometimes the battles no one knows about ask us to stop. To turn towards them. To acknowledge what is actually happening. And, most importantly, to let ourselves be witnessed.

This men's circle is an invitation to bring one of those lone-fought battles into the room.

We'll explore

  • What battle you are currently carrying
  • Why you have been fighting it alone
  • What happens when we keep difficult things to ourselves
  • The stories we carry about strength, weakness and asking for help
  • What it means to be witnessed without being fixed
  • The difference between vulnerability and weakness
  • What support actually looks like
  • Where you might be holding tension, resistance or emotion in the body
  • What you need from yourself — and perhaps from others
  • And what might change when you allow someone else to stand beside you

Through embodiment, reflection, honest conversation and creativity, we'll explore the experience of carrying something alone, and what it feels like if we don't have to.

We'll say

"This is what I'm dealing with."
"This is what I've been carrying."
"This is the part I haven't told anyone."

This circle is for you if there is something in your life that you've been navigating quietly.

This is a confidential, supportive space for men to show up honestly, listen deeply, and discover what happens when something that has been carried alone is finally shared.

Come as you are.
Bring what you're carrying.
Bring the battle.
Bring the uncertainty.

You may have been fighting it alone, but you don't have to meet it alone.

We'll sit with it together.

An evening of embodiment, reflection, creativity, honest conversation, presence and connection.

About your Facilitator Jozef Popjak

Jozef is a trained architect and offers coaching to create your own universe, bridging design with human potential and transformation though coaching, facilitation and men's work. He trained with the Co-Active Training Institute (CTI), FLOW Coaching Institute, and PQ Coaching. He now partners with individuals and teams, facilitating learning, transitions, and growth, helping people and organisations thrive from the inside out.

His work is rooted in a calm presence, shaped by years of mindfulness, meditation, and embodiment practices. Guided by natural curiosity and a passion for art and innovation, he recognises and works with the intricate structures and patterns that influence our spaces, minds, and lives.
