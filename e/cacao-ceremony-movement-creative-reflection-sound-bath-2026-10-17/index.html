Cacao Ceremony, Movement, Creative Reflection & Sound Bath 7.30-9.30pm A nourishing evening gathering with ceremonial cacao, guided journeying, elemental rhythm and movement, creative journalling and a deeply immersive sound bath. Join us for an evening of awareness, intention, rebalance & inner alignment, renewing & connecting. Doors open 7.15pm — please arrive on time for a 7.30pm start. Together, cacao and sound offer a powerful conduit for insight, renewal and embodied presence.—inviting you to pause, reconnect with inner wisdom, and create intentions for the way ahead.Come into resonance with a growing community supporting conscious choice — enjoy a delicious cacao ceremony inviting awareness, clarity and intention, supported with rhythmic movement to integrate embodied wisdom, and relax into a deeply nourishing and restorative sound journey to support balance and nervous system reset. What to Expect Opening Circle — we gather in circle to arrive, meet and create a shared container of presence, trust and intention. Cacao Ceremony & Guided Journey — serving ceremonial cacao and guiding into the heart of awareness, supporting grounding, clarity and intention-setting for the evening. Rhythm of our ancestors — inviting movement as your body integrates your inner knowing, ceremonial cacao, and prepares for our sound journey. SoulAlign Visioning & Creative Journalling — we’ll explore what is calling for renewal, and conscious embodied choice. Sound Journey & Inspiration Integration — an immersive sound bath integrate clarity through deep rest and relaxation. This extended sound journey allows the body and mind to settle, balance and integrate this evening experience. Closing circle — we close the evening with clarity for onward choice. What to Bring • A Journal and a pen • Comfortable, warm clothing • Drinking water • The venue has blankets, cushions, & yoga mats - you're welcome to bring your own. About Your Hosts Anna Ma — Anam Cara Anna is a guide for soul-led transformation, supporting people to reconnect with inner clarity, embodied choice and meaningful direction. With over 20 years’ experience in personal development, nervous-system-awareness and realignment, Anna guides inner processes, and creates grounded space for insight, integration and transformation. Blending cacao ceremony with transformational coaching, intuitive guidance, creative reflection and embodied presence —Anna supports clients to access inner wisdom, navigate life transitions and move into embodiment beyond distraction. https://www.anna-ma.life/cacao https://www.anna-ma.life/coaching https://www.instagram.com/i_anna_ma_/ Chai Francesco Chai is a holistic therapist with a background in music, holding a Master’s degree from the Guildhall School of Music & Drama. He hosts regular sound healing sessions at Zen Yoga Studio London.With over a decade of experience in sound therapy, Chai qualified with a Diploma in Sound Therapy from the Sound Healing Academy in 2015 and has since been offering both group and one-to-one sound bath sessions across London. Chai also facilitates workshops in meditation and self-healing, guiding participants on a deeply relaxing journey through the healing power of sound. Using crystal singing bowls and oriental gongs, each session becomes a sanctuary of calm, rest, and renewal.Chai's sessions invite you to reconnect with your deep self, creating space away from the noise and pace of modern, digital life. https://www.instagram.com/chai_sound_massage/ https://www.chaisoundhealing.com/ About Ceremonial Cacao Ceremonial-grade cacao is a natural, unprocessed plant elixir traditionally used to support clarity, heart openness and connection. Unlike everyday chocolate, it is gently prepared to retain its natural properties and consumed with intention.Cacao used in ceremony is always organically and ethically sourced through native tribespeople in the South Americas, supporting fair trade, communities and traditional growing practices. Important Information • Cacao contains small amounts of caffeine and theobromine - if you are sensitive to stimulants, you may prefer a smaller portion • If you have a serious heart condition, are pregnant, or are taking antidepressant medication, it is not advised to drink cacao - please consult your healthcare provider • Please inform us if you have allergies to cacao or chocolate You are welcome to participate without consuming cacao if preferred.
