Delicious Pleasures is an indulgent girls’ night in for women (cis, trans and AFAB), exploring pleasure in all its forms, outside of the bedroom. Come for cocktails, canapés, conversation and connection, and get curious about what pleasure means to you when you put yourself at the centre of your own experience. What to expect Meaningful conversation and connection with like-minded women Mini masterclasses on living deliciously, sensual food and feminine magnetism Playful exploration of dressing and expressing yourself for yourself Opportunities to practise asking for what you want, receiving and taking up more space Beautiful canapés from private chef Niki Merali Cocktails, refreshments and alcohol-free options A carefully curated goodie bag to take home A small number of carefully selected male valets, offering opportunities to explore receiving attention, appreciation and adoration. Participation is always optional. No sexual play. There is no partner or previous experience required, and you can participate as much or as little as feels right for you. The evening is designed to be welcoming, respectful and consent-led. What to wear: whatever makes you feel good. There is no strict dress code.
