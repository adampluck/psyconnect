Join us for Deep Listening Room w/ Desirée

ABOUT ARTIST Influenced by the underground scene, Italian-born and London-based DJ Desireè gets deeply inspired by the frequencies and connections one can experience on the dance floor. Founder of Extraterrestrial Desire, a podcast channel focused on sci-fi, ambient, and electronic soundscapes, and co-founder of the London-based collective Polyritmica, she showcases her passion and dedication to the scene.

This, combined with her carefully curated selections and reputation as an artist capable of enchanting crowds, has secured her spots on lineups across the globe at some of the best underground clubs, such as Fold, Tresor, and TES (Tbilisi). She has shared the booth with artists like DVS1, Ø [Phase], Amotik, Ben Klock, Rødhåd, Kerrie, Jin Synth, and Psyk, and has played numerous Unfolds, as well as Ben Klock's very own Photon afterparty and Ignez's Somov label showcase.

Deep and mesmerising organic textures combine with atmospheric, alien-like, loopy energy and pounding 4x4 rhythms. It's what defines Desireè's sets. Expect full hypnosis, a dive into raw emotions and tactile sensations.

WHAT IS DEEP LISTENING? These special sessions are designed to allow you to experience sound without any distractions. We curate this chamber for complete audio immersion with our hi-fidelity sound system and incredible intentional DJs sets on select weekends, allowing you to explore all the textures, layers and dimensions of electronic music, while you lay down undistracted by the world around you. A simple concept, yet rare to find in London.

There is also a collective element to deep listening. By listening together we give each other more agency to fully drop into the listening experience and in doing so, discover deeper profound ways sound can leave an impression on us.

These experiences can help you feel more present, they allow our minds room to drift, imagine, remember, dream - all very nourishing and relaxing to our nervous system. Trust that at Om Being we want you to be walking away feeling truly moved by the experience.
