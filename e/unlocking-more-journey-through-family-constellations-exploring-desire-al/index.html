Journey through Family Constellations exploring desire, alchemising resistance and opening to possibility

A one-day constellation experience with Imani Faith

There is more you want from this life. What keeps you from claiming it?

Perhaps you’re ready for deeper love, greater visibility, a different relationship with money, or the freedom to follow a calling that won’t leave you alone. You can feel the desire. Yet each time you move towards it, something pulls you back.

On Unlocking More, we’ll get curious about that pull. What looks like resistance or self-sabotage may have a deeper story. This is a day to bring what you’re longing for into the room and discover what stands beside it and what happens when that part is brought into awareness.

What happens in a constellation?

Systemic Family Constellations is a live, experiential process of entering a new paradigm within your life. Someone brings a question about their life, and other people in the circle may be invited to represent parts of it: a person, a relationship, a feeling, an obstacle or a possibility. As they find their places, dynamics that were hard to name can become visible and shifts start to occur naturally. I guide the constellation, following what emerges rather than deciding in advance where it should go.

It doesn’t have to be a family issue. You can bring a question about love, money, work, purpose, belonging or your next chapter. We start there and see where the entanglement is.

We’ll work together in one shared circle. I’ll introduce the practice, guide the constellations and leave space to reflect on what the day opens up. You may offer a question to be considered for a constellation, represent in someone else’s, or witness. You choose how you participate. The field works it's gifts upon us all.

What are you ready to stop circling around and begin moving towards?

No previous experience needed. This is a space that welcomes all, just as you are.

About Imani Faith

My practice is rooted in Systemic Family Constellations and shaped through advanced training, supervision, international study and years spent exploring story, spirituality and human behaviour.

My training follows a lineage connected to the development of Family Constellations through Bert Hellinger, alongside contemporary approaches exploring ancestry, embodiment, consciousness and the field. International study has taken me into rooms with practitioners and teachers from across the world, including the International Constellations Intensive in Sintra, Portugal.

My curiosity reaches beyond one discipline. Into consciousness and epigenetics. Quantum physics and the nature of reality. Ancestry and embodiment. Spirituality, intuition and the subtle dimensions of human experience.

I am fascinated by what we inherit. By what we sense. By what remains unseen. By the relationship between the systems behind us and the life waiting ahead of us. And by the moment something that has quietly shaped a life is finally seen.

Because what we can see, we can begin to meet differently.

Special Guest

Sophia Kai - Sophia is a multilingual writer and musician from Sri Lankan, Greek and East African heritage. Her art is widely known for exploring the depth, contradiction and vulnerability of the human experience while articulating resistance to systems that constrain dignity, memory and freedom, both inwardly and socially. Sophia’s work encourages a more deliberate relationship with music and art, rooted in sustained listening rather than fast consumption.

Supported by

Aliya Lily - Aliya is a singer-songwriter whose music explores the depth, tenderness and beauty of the human experience. Rooted in love, nature and a life lived between cultures, her work is inspired by the many landscapes that have shaped her. She joins Family Constellation as part of the supporting team, and offers soundscapes to accompany the journey.

Farah Orths - Farah Orths is a Hay House Author, channel, ceremonialist, and musician who brings the unconditional love of the Divine into the human realm as an awakening force of healing. Her teachings invite radical softening into receiving God's holding and guidance just as you are. Her work is centred around our relationship to receiving your innate abundance and wholeness and moving into the world from this place.
