Good Company with Ignacio Ramos

Explore non-dual teachings and Rumi's mysticism with Professor Ignacio Ramos at Colet House.

Join us in Person at Colet House or register below to receive the Zoom meeting link. Meeting schedule: 6.00–6.30: Chi Kung — Daniel Brierley (facilitating via Zoom) 6.30–7.00: Shared silent meditation 7.00–8.00: Voyage of Discovery — Ignacio Ramos (facilitating in person) Professor at the National Autonomous University of Mexico in the Department of Psychosocial and Cultural Processes. Member of the Discourse Analysis and Semiotics of Culture Faculty. He has conducted studies with native peoples of Mexico in the areas of health and conception of the human body. In recent years, he has delved into Eastern Sufi thought. He published in Spanish the translation of Book 1 of Jalaluddin Rumi’s Masnavi, translated from the Persian by Alan Williams. We welcome all who wish to learn and share non-dual teachings to enhance inner peace, embrace fresh ideas and make new connections. Currently presented both on zoom and live at our home, Colet House, Barons Court, the evenings open with a simple practice such as kirtan, chair yoga or breathing exercises to prepare for a shared half hour’s silent meditation followed by a presentation. To register, just click the link below and you will receive an invitation to join. https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/tZYvc-qrrzIqHdaIYiCwcSyRDZGbLfOmkPgo Long term Study Society member, designer and artist Jonathan Leiserach invites presenters from various disciplines including philosophers, scientists, doctors, musicians, artists, writers, and spiritual teachers to share thoughts and experience on non-dual themes. Afterwards, for those who come to Colet House, there is the opportunity to join The Movements to Music classes which The Study Society has offered for over 70 years.
