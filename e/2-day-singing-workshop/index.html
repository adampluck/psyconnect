2-DAY SINGING WORKSHOP IN LONDON Weekend singing workshops are an opportunity to meet within a song, creating a space for free creativity for those, who have a natural need of vocal expression and would like to start (or continue) an adventure with the voice and traditional songs, as a material for work. more about the workshops: http://laboratoriumpiesni.pl/en/workshops Workshops are focused on: 1. Group practise of music and traditional singing (working with traditional songs, learning singing in traditional polyphony); 2. Phenomenon of voice and its strengthening (emission, voice projection, breath training); 3. Awakening of the natural human musicality (the musical sensation of the world); 4. Partner and group activities (building a state of mindfulness, mutual respect and responsibility for common work, ability to fit to the group, creating a creative energy, mutual support and exploiting the potential of each participant). - The workshop will be led by: Kamila Bigus - a violinist, singer musical arranger in Laboratorium Pieśni, graduate of the Stanislaw Moniuszko State Academy of Music in Gdansk at the violin class, graduate of the Academy of Fine Arts in Gdansk (Interdepartmental Institute of Art), co-leader of Song Laboratory Workshops; participated in theatre plays and a series of the audiovisual performances ‘Understanding Poland’ with the Łukasz Rostkowski’s (L.U.C) chamber music ensemble; a session musician; took part in many CD recordings of the artists, such as: Symphonic Theater of Dreams, Miuosh, False Month, Crane days, Miejscovi.
