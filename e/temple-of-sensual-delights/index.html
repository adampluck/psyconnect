Temple of Sensual Delights Learn the art of sensual pleasure — how to offer it, how to give it, and how to consciously receive it. This is one of our larger gatherings. Come and join 50 wonderful human beings all curious about the same thing: what happens when pleasure stops being an afterthought and becomes something we practice, on purpose, together. Experience pleasure as a force for good Pleasure isn't indulgence. It's not something to earn after everything else is done. Approached with intention, it becomes medicine — rippling outward into how we relate, how we show up, how we move through the rest of our lives. This evening is an invitation: to uncover your sensual side, and let it take you somewhere you didn't know was possible. What to expect Our signature Soma & Eros facilitation drawing upon on years of psychology & therapy, tao tantric practice, and deep relational work. You'll be guided throughout the evening, held by facilitators and a team who is there to support you. Who this is for Anyone curious about deepening their relationship with pleasure, connection, and their own sensuality — whether you're completely new to this world or have been exploring it for years. Pleasure, approached this way, becomes something bigger than the evening itself — it ripples outward into how you relate, how you show up, how you move through the rest of your life. All experience levels welcome When you join the waitlist, you will receive an email within a day or two, with further details. This event has three ticket tiers. Apply via this link to join our community and we will send you a link to a vetting call. The process includes an approx. 20 minutes online call with one of the organisers. We talk about your experience in S+ spaces, intention for joining, anything we need to be aware of to support you in the space, checking your ID, and you can ask us any questions you might have. The vetting call has a small fee of £10 which gives you a similar credit towards a temple ticket. Facilitated by: Roy Graff is a life and relationship coach who draws on his training in Psychosynthesis psychotherapy, Radical Honesty, Non-violent Communication and Wheel of Consent training to offer coaching and mentoring to individuals, couples and polycules. Roy has facilitated a range of workshops involving non-monogamy, Polyamory, kink, consent and boundaries in London, Berlin and at various festivals and retreats. Envokari is a healer and sacred sexuality guide. Building on her formal training as a psychologist, and the Taotantric arts, she holds a profound space for healing and transformation.
