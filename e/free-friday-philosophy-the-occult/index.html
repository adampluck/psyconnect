Free Friday Philosophy: The Occult

Explore magic, mysticism and Neoplatonism with Philosophy Cafe London in Holborn.

When
Friday 30 October 2026 · 18:50
Where
London, UK

Philosophy Cafe London **Free Friday Philosophy** is an experiment jointly hosted by **Veins of London** and **Philosophy Cafe London**. The idea is to bring our two communities together for an evening of philosophy that is completely free to attend. We hope it might lead to further Free Friday Philosophy events, but for now we’re seeing how this first one goes.

As our first event falls on the night before Halloween, **The Occult** was an irresistible choice of subject.

We normally explore what might be called the official history of Western philosophy. This evening, we will look at its unofficial or shadow history: magic, mysticism, Kabbalah, alchemy and the search for hidden knowledge.

We will also explore the perhaps surprising links between these traditions and ideas now regarded as part of orthodox philosophy. At the centre of the evening will be the influence of Plato and Neoplatonism: the idea that the visible world reflects a higher spiritual reality, and that knowledge can lead us towards it.

We will follow some of these ideas through the Kabbalah, John Dee, the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley, before considering their influence on modern paganism and Wicca.

After a short introduction, participants will propose and vote on questions to discuss in small groups before sharing their thoughts with the whole room.

You do not need to have studied philosophy or arrive with specialist knowledge. Curiosity and a willingness to join the dialogue are enough.

**Where:** Downstairs room, Square Pig, Holborn (private room)

**NB**: The event begins at 7pm, doors open at 6:50pm. Event ends at 9pm

**Format:** Participants propose questions → vote on questions → small-group discussions → share insights with the whole room

**Price: Free**
