Come for the curiosity, stay for the connections! 🌿✨

Looking for meaningful conversations, new friendships and a community of curious, open-minded people?

Join the London Psychedelic Community for a relaxed Sunday afternoon where fascinating ideas, different perspectives and genuine connections come together.

We'll have our own private room at Sir Richard Steele in Belsize Park, creating a welcoming space to meet new people, explore life's bigger questions and enjoy good company.

Expect thought-provoking conversations, playful icebreakers, our very own conversation cards and opportunities to meet people you might never otherwise cross paths with.

Whether you're a seasoned psychonaut, a curious newcomer or simply someone fascinated by life's bigger questions, everyone is welcome!

Coming alone? You're in good company! Many people attend on their own, and our activities make it easy to break the ice and feel included.

You never know which conversation might spark a new perspective, a lasting friendship or something wonderful! 💚

🎟️ Limited seating! As we have a private room with limited capacity, we recommend booking your ticket early to secure your place.

✨ What to Expect

A friendly, informal gathering where conversations might explore:

🌈 Psychedelics, altered states, research and harm reduction
🧠 Consciousness, psychology, neuroscience and mental wellbeing
🔮 World religions and belief systems, spirituality, mysticism, esotericism and philosophy
🌱 Personal growth, self-exploration, wellbeing and integration
🌌 Science, nature, the seen and unseen worlds, and the mysteries of existence

These are just some of the subjects that bring our community together. Conversations are free to flow wherever curiosity takes us!

No specialist knowledge or experience is needed — just curiosity and an open mind.

💭 A Taste of the Conversations

Our specially designed conversation cards invite you to explore fascinating questions and exchange perspectives in a relaxed, playful way.

For example

🌌 What if everything we perceive is only a tiny fraction of what actually exists?

🤝 What makes you feel genuinely connected to another person?

From the profound and philosophical to the playful and unexpected, there's always something interesting to explore!

You don't need to have all the answers. Sometimes the most fascinating conversations begin with a simple question.

🤝 Icebreakers, Conversations & New Connections

We'll ease into a mix of our very own conversation cards, Find Your Tribe Bingo, playful icebreakers and small-group rotations, designed to spark curiosity, move beyond everyday small talk and help everyone connect.

You'll have opportunities to meet different people, discover shared interests, exchange perspectives and enjoy meaningful conversations.

✨ Here's a rough outline of the afternoon

3:00–3:15 PM | Arrivals & Introductions
Settle in, grab a drink and meet a few friendly faces.

Discover shared interests and make new connections.

Reconnect, mingle and let conversations develop naturally.

The atmosphere will be relaxed and informal, with just enough structure to help everyone feel included, especially newcomers.

Join in however you feel comfortable. You're welcome to join the games, enjoy one-to-one conversations, mingle freely or simply soak up the atmosphere. Timings are flexible, and there's no pressure to participate, speak in front of others or share anything personal.

Food and drinks, including non-alcoholic options, are available to purchase separately. Feel free to arrive hungry or simply enjoy a drink while getting to know everyone.

🌿 About Your Host

Hi, I'm Sinbad! 👋

I use the pseudonym Sinbad to maintain my professional privacy. I'm an experienced, trauma-informed educator specialising in Social, Emotional and Mental Health education, with a background in mathematics and Telecommunications Engineering.

I'm also the organiser and host of the London Psychedelic Community, bringing people together since 2018 through socials, thought-provoking conversations and shared experiences.

I'm a philomath at heart, a lifelong lover of learning with an insatiable curiosity about the universe, the human experience and the mysteries of existence. My interests span psychedelics, consciousness, science, psychology, mathematics, sacred geometry, cymatics, spirituality, philosophy, world religions and belief systems, mysticism, esotericism, eschatology and ancient wisdom.

I'm particularly drawn to spiritual healing and naturopathy, and the different ways people explore healing, wellbeing and the connection between mind, body and spirit. I'm also fascinated by where these diverse perspectives intersect and the questions they raise about the seen and unseen worlds. Nature holds a special place in my heart, and I find endless beauty, mystery and inspiration in the natural world.

Above all, what I enjoy most is connecting with people, hearing their stories and creating welcoming spaces where strangers can become friends.

Whether you're joining us for the first time, feeling a little shy or you're already a familiar face, please come and say hello! I'm always happy to make introductions and help you feel at home.

Looking forward to meeting you! 🌿✨
