TEMPLE OF LIFE & DEATH A journey into the realms of life and death. We will enter the mystery of what it means to live, to desire, to lose, to surrender and to die. Through a transformational death ceremony and immersive experiences, we will journey through the depths of desire, identity, ego, attachment, grief, aliveness and rebirth. This is not about becoming a better version of yourself. This is about something deeper. What are you willing to let die? The identity you have built. The story you keep telling. The ego that needs to be right, seen or in control. The armour that keeps love at a distance. The need to be seen a certain way. The desire to control. The attachment to who you think you are. What remains when there is nothing left to hold, protect or defend? Death teaches us how to live. And so we will end the journey in a celebration of life and the extraordinary gift of being alive.
