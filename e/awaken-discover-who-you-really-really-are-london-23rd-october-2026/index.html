Together we’ll be deconstructing the rational mind and exploring the power of first-person experience to reveal the unconditional loving presence that radiates at the heart of all experience, that animates not only our human self but the entirety of this here now. Other lines of inquiry may include: what is the impact of this experiential realisation? Does it naturally lead to personal transformation? How does it relate to the Circling and Surrendered Leadership practices? The evening will not have a formal structure, but will instead be conducted in the spirit of surrender to what emerges from within the group.
