THOUGHT EXPERIMENTS IN PUBLIC (TEiP) **A free day retreat under the trees, co-produced by our community.**

For all the up to date info and exact location, here's our **[DIGITAL ZINE](https://thoughtexperiments.kit.com/teipdayretreat-october2026)**

We held the first TEiP Day Retreat in August and it was a great success! So we're tempting fate and doing it again in October. This is a fair-weather event. If it rains, we will cancel the retreat and hang out at the Southbank instead.

This time, we have an even bigger line up of activities, so please join us in praying for dry weather!

**How does a outdoor day retreat connect to TEiP (THOUGHT EXPERIMENTS IN PUBLIC)?** TEiP has become a thriving community because our events create opportunities for people to get straight into meaningful, imaginative, creative conversations.

The Day Retreats are opportunities to take that imagination and creativity and sense of community to the next level.

They are an opportunity for people to share and learn from one another in the same openminded, relaxed spirit as TEiP.

It's outside because we love nature and our last outside event attracted a huge number of RSVPs demonstrating major enthusiasm for outdoor gatherings.

Of course, October weather is unpredictable. If it rains heavily, we will cancel the retreat and instead arrange an informal hang out at the Southbank.

**How will it work?**

* We'll gather in one of London's pockets of forest (Epping Forest) for a day. * There will be a central hub for '[hanging out](https://experimentsingroupwisdom.substack.com/p/hanging-out)' and the occasional TEiP style round of conversation. * In nearby clearings and 'rooms of the forest', throughout the day, there will be optional activities hosted by community members (e.g. improv, forest bathing, poetry, origami, etc.) * It will all be free!

**Please [get in touch](https://www.alltogether.one/contact) if you'd like to run an activity 😊**
