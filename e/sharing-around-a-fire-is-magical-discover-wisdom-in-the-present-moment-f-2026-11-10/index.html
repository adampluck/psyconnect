*Welcome to compassionate sharing circle.*

Come *home to yourself* and your body to “wholeness,” “connectedness” then to be *seen and heard* fully. Connect, in a *heart led* space where we grow in compassion with ourselves and others. Releases our *spontaneous and authentic selves, *let’s find peace in the middle the storm. Experience the *opening to live fully* in the present moment.

A Sharing Circle is an *ancient* system to foster *interconnectedness* with others. In the circle we foster trust, inclusion and wholeness.

*“As someone who has never enjoyed being part of this circle, it was the first time that I felt like there was space for me. Space for me to show up as me, but more importantly, space for me to speak, I started to feel extraordinary”*

*Have you ever thought something is missing? Sharing Circles, are part of our shared lost heritage. Come, return to a natural elemental way to being in the flow, then, when we listen deeply we listen with only one purpose: to help us to empty our hearts.

We will sit outside together with fire and all the elements and draw awareness into the body. Intimacy begins with our willingness to see clearly - *beyond our separation*. We learn how to *“be with”* all aspects of ourselves without needing to, fix, solve, or change anything, when we hold a deep love and acceptance, then we understand our extraordinary nature.

*Location* Plot 9 Maze Hill station.The centre-piece of is the Seated Parachute Space (aka The Dodeca-Den). The Address: (Opposite) Leamington Court, Restell Close, Greenwich, London SE3 7RD. Google maps pin. The best way of getting here is to head to Maze Hill Station. If you’re driving then there’s usually plenty of free space on nearby Humber Road (2hrs allowed, unrestricted after 6:30pm – so effectively free from 4:30pm).

*Accessibility* Unfortunately there is no wheelchair access as the ground is sloped. If I can assist at all, I would be most happy.

Any questions please call Harold Now on 0757 4185 624 i’d love to hear your voice. I’ll call you back if I’m not available.

The introduction will be about 10 min. There will be some time of silence or guided meditation to start and end. The rest is circle sharing time.

*What is group Facilitation*? Facilitation creates a safe space and then guides a process for direct alive communication enabling a positive participatory climate for transformation and self realisation. It “enables powerful change” by making people feel worthwhile, included connected and valued. I ask you to listen deeply and stay present with active listening.

We believe in No unquestionable truth. We espouse no religion. I practice daily *wholeness, gratitude, connectedness, and compassion*. We walk this path and move through all our mistakes by deepening compassion, trustworthiness and transparency.
