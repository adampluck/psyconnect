LiveRhythms is our 5Rhythms dance amplified by the soul and spontaneity of live musicians. In these sessions, the music and the dancers breathe together. The drums, strings, and melodies respond to our movement in real-time, creating a deep, resonant space for us to lose ourselves and find our rhythm. This is a high-vibe, heart-opening journey. Whether the music is a gentle heartbeat or a wild, driving crescendo, it is all designed to support your personal dance and our collective connection. The Experience: The Sound: A curated blend of live instruments and soulful rhythms tailored to the 5Rhythms wave. The Space: The same warm, welcoming Kew community, with a little extra spark in the air. Arrival: Doors open at 7:15 PM. We recommend arriving early to settle into the space and the sound. Ease & Access: Exchange: Sliding scale (scroll down or click Register). Travel: Easy access via Kew Gardens station and free parking directly outside the hall. No prior experience with live music or 5Rhythms is necessary. Just bring your body and an open heart.
