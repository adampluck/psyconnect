Teatime Concert

Young virtuosos from RAM and RCM perform chamber music with tea and cake in an intimate West London setting.

Where

Price

Host

‘Teatime Concert’ on Saturday 24 October Back by popular demand, Colet House welcomes Young Artist Musical Ambassadors (YAMA) of the Music Mind Spirit Trust , in collaboration with their impressively talented colleagues from the Junior Academy, Royal Academy of Music. This will be a wonderful and refreshing opportunity to hear beautiful music and inspirational playing at its best. This delightful programme will include violinists J oe Netley (Royal College of Music) , Vera Hsuan (Junior Academy, RAM) and other musicians selected by JA Head of Performance Dan Tong and violin professor Chika Robertson. Doors will open at 3.45, with tea from 4 , followed by this enjoyable concert from 5-6 pm . For any members of Colet House or the public who would like to sing in the newly formed Colet Chorus for the ‘Power of Music II’ concert at Colet House on Sunday 1 November, there will be an opportunity to have a short run-through of the featured choral piece with Dr Mark Ross Clark from 6.15 – 7 pm at Colet house after the concert. Ticket price includes tea and cake. All proceeds and donations received will support musical scholarships and performances for the young musicians. Tickets: Adults £15 (seniors £12) Children / students £5
