Join us for Deep Listening Room with Kate Fleur Young

Kate Fleur Young is a London-based artist who is known for her captivating live performances that incorporate ritual and ceremony, inspiring audiences to reconnect with their inner landscapes and to Nature.

Her mesmerising voice intertwined with the sound of the magical alchemy crystal bowls and electronic soundscapes are a spiritual vibration and highlight the interconnection between the natural world and the cyclical rhythms of life, dissolving the bridge of separation and providing a musical sanctuary where people can heal and find balance and harmony in a fast-paced world filled with stress and overwhelm

Kate has performed in leading venues and festivals including London’s Barbican Centre, Alexandra Palace, eartH Hackney, Wilderness, The Round Chapel, Hougton, Medicine Festival and 180 Strand. Her multidisciplinary projects span installations at the Saatchi Gallery, the V&A and The Strand. She has created spundscapes for Marshmallow Laser Feast, released a Sound Journey on Radio 1, and been included in the Beckley Foundation’s psychedelic playlists.

This deep listening session is a sonic journey through the sacred landscapes of the British Isles. Through layered vocal worlds, immersive electronic textures, and field recordings gathered from the land, Kate creates a living soundscape that invites the listener into a deeply embodied experience of place. Crystal bowl frequencies and resonant tones gently guide the mind into alpha and theta brainwave states, opening space for rest, reflection, and subtle transformation. This immersive experience allows the listener to traverse ancient landscapes through sound, where inner and outer worlds begin to soften, and a sense of peace can emerge through connection with Natture - felt through mind, body, and spirit.
