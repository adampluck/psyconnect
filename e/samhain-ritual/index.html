Honouring Death, Life and the InBetween. 31 Oct 2026, 19:00 – 22:00 OMUK, Unit 1, 22 Pakenham St, London WC1X 0LB, UK For Samhain this year we are offering a special Ritual to honour Death, Life and the InBetween. Life & Death are interconnected, they are inseparable. You cannot have one without the other. On the 31st October we celebrate both worlds. We honour those that are no longer with us, those that have passed over and those we wish to remember. This auspicious date is when the veil between the two worlds is at its thinnest, when communication and connection with the 'Other Side' is most accessible. Guiding us through the ritual we have Mark Estdale (founder of OMUK) offering voice activation and gong and Felix Oram (Temenos founder) providing faciliation and music. The ritual will include an opening circle to set intentions to prepare us as we dive deep into the depths of our psyche to honour the symbolic, symbiotic and transformational powers of death and life. Places are strictly limited to 20. Doors open: 7pm Session: 7.30pm - 10pm
