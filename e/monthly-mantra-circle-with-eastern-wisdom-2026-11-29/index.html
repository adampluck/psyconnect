A sacred circle of open-hearted souls seeking conscious conversation, exploration of eastern spirituality and to experience the uplifting power of kirtan, aka mantra meditation: connecting to our deeper self, the greater whole and source through sound and sacred syllables.

In the ancient Sanskrit language, such a gathering is described as Sanga, which means wholesome and uplifting community. Finding your high vibrational tribe and spending quality time exploring transformational spirituality together. We learn from the wisdom already contained within each of us and from that of the ancients. Supported through storytelling and journalling and rounding off with vegan food.
