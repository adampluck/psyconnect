FOLLOW THE START TO EAST LONDON!

This festive season, we're bringing together three modern-day Wise Men for an extraordinary gathering at LoveShack, East London, where ancient wisdom meets sacred plants, psychedelic history, and the mysteries of Christmas!

Forget the usual gold, frankincense, and myrrh. Our three Magi - Shehbaz Khan, Danny Nemu, and Darren Le Baron - are bringing their own gifts of knowledge, research, and personal experience to unwrap some of the most fascinating, forgotten, and controversial stories surrounding the festive season.

We've all heard the story of the Three Wise Men who followed a star to Bethlehem, bearing gifts for the newborn Jesus. But who were these mysterious visitors from the East? What was the significance of their offerings? And could the origins of some of our most cherished Christmas traditions be connected to sacred plants, psychoactive substances, and ancient spiritual practices?

From the aromatic resins of the ancient world to psychoactive plants in biblical traditions, flying reindeer, magical mushrooms, and the mysteries of Santa Claus, we'll explore the stories behind the stories.

Were the Three Wise Men really kings, or could they have been ancient drug runners? And what exactly was in Santa's sack? 🎅🏾🍄

Join us for an evening of thought-provoking presentations, alternative perspectives, lively discussion, and a healthy dose of festive mischief!

Three Wise Men. Three unique offerings. One unforgettable journey into the mysteries of Christmas.

THREE WISE MEN. THREE SACRED GIFTS. ONE FESTIVE JOURNEY.

Whether you're fascinated by ancient history, psychedelic research, religious mythology, sacred plants, or simply curious about the origins of Christmas, this gathering promises to offer a fresh perspective on the festive season.

Bring your curiosity, your questions, and an open mind as we follow the star and explore the mysteries of Christmas together.

Places are limited, secure your spot and join the gathering!

MEET THE THREE WISE MEN

SHEHBAZ KHAN

The Sacred Mysteries of Frankincense

Founder of The Frankincense Store, Shehbaz Khan has spent over 15 years exploring and sharing the extraordinary world of frankincense.

His journey began in the bustling markets of Oman, where one encounter with the heavenly aroma of burning frankincense sparked a lifelong fascination. Drawing upon traditional Bedouin knowledge, personal spiritual experiences, and years of research, Shehbaz has dedicated himself to exploring the cultural, medicinal, and spiritual significance of this ancient resin.

This Christmas, Shehbaz brings the gift of frankincense, revealing the fascinating history, varieties, traditional uses, and sacred symbolism of one of the most mysterious offerings presented to the infant Jesus.

What made frankincense so precious, and why was it considered a gift fit for the divine?

Prepare to follow your nose into the aromatic mysteries of the ancient world!

DANNY NEMU

The Three Wise Men: Kings or Drug Runners?

Researcher, author, and psychedelic scholar Danny Nemu is no stranger to challenging conventional interpretations of ancient religious texts.

Best known for his provocative research into psychoactive substances in biblical history, Danny is the author of Getting High with the Most High and the creator of the fascinating Drugs in the Bible presentations.

From ancient Hebrew scriptures to the mysterious gifts of the Magi, Danny explores the possible connections between sacred substances, altered states of consciousness, and religious experience.

This festive season, he'll be unwrapping one of his most controversial questions:

Were the Three Wise Men really kings, or were they travelling merchants carrying some of the ancient world's most sought-after psychoactive substances?

Expect an entertaining and thought-provoking journey through biblical history, sacred chemistry, and the mysteries of the Magi!

DARREN LE BARON

The Psychedelic Mysteries of Christmas

Spore-spreading educator, ethnomycologist, and psychedelic researcher Darren Le Baron is known for reminding anyone who will listen that "You Are a Mushroom Having a Human Experience!"

But what happens when we turn that mushroom-shaped lens towards Christmas?

Have you ever wondered why Santa wears red and white? Where the stories of flying reindeer originated? Why Christmas trees are decorated with colourful ornaments? Or whether certain festive traditions have connections to ancient shamanic practices and psychoactive mushrooms?

Drawing upon ethnomycology, mythology, folklore, and comparative religion, Darren explores the fascinating—and often debated—theories connecting Amanita muscaria mushrooms, winter solstice celebrations, Indigenous traditions, and the modern mythology of Christmas.

From the birth of Christ to Santa's magical journey across the night sky, Darren invites you to question what we think we know about the festive season, distinguishing historical traditions from modern interpretations and psychedelic speculation.

Could there be more to the Christmas story than meets the eye? And have mushrooms been hiding beneath the Christmas tree all along?

Come discover the mysteries for yourself!
