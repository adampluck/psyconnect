Fascia Maneuvers by Human Garage  Workshop – March Tuesday Series Ready to unlock stuck trauma & energy from your body? With Human Garage Fascia Maneuvers, you can master a quiet mind and rejuvenated body! Fascial Maneuver Workshop- Guided by Human Garage Certified Coach Join us for a restorative Fascia Maneuver Workshop — a guided journey of movement, breath, and awareness designed to release stored tension and trauma within the body’s connective tissue. Combining posture, breathwork, and mindful intention, this experience helps realign the body, expand mobility, and encourage emotional balance. Discover how gentle, self-applied techniques can unlock your body’s natural flow and restore harmony from the inside out. What are Fascia Maneuvers? Fascial Maneuvers, created by Human Garage, are self-applied movements & breathwork techniques that release tension and trauma stored in the fascia—the connective tissue around muscles and organs. Combining posture, breath, intention, motion, they restore alignment, mobility, emotional balance. Why participate in this Fascia Maneuver workshop? Release built-up tension and trauma held in the body’s connective tissues, restore natural alignment, and support emotional release and flow. What to expect? Participants will practice guided self-movements, breathwork, posture shifts, and intention to gradually unlock fascial restrictions and expand mobility in a safe, supportive setting.  PLEASE BRING A YOGA MAT, WATER and Curious Mind! Precautions and Limitations: Not suitable during acute injury, open wounds, deep vein thrombosis, unstable spine, or for those with conditions such as uncontrolled hypertension or severe osteoporosis. Please consult your healthcare provider if pregnant or managing serious health issues.
