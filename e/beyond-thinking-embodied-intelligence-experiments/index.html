Beyond Thinking: Embodied Intelligence Experiments

Test whether moving your body unlocks better solutions than pure debate and reasoning.

When
Tuesday 22 September 2026 · 19:00
Where
Newspeak House (Newspeak Hall), 133-135 Bethnal Green Road, London E2 7DG
Host
Euan McLean

healing & wellbeing

Can embodiment improve your effectiveness at solving problems? Let’s find out.

In this workshop, we’ll explore a real-world scenario twice. First, the way we already know: reasoning, debating, building the case. Then a second time instead using an embodied role-play practice.

This is an experiment to help us understand the strengths of each mode & how to flow between them.

Logistics:

This is the first of three experimental sessions. Come curious and willing to move. Bring a journal and some examples of difficult situations you’d like fresh perspectives on.

Doors open at 19:00, please arrive by 19:30 to ensure a closed container (you will not be able to participate if you arrive later than 19:30). The workshop will run from 19:30-21:30.
