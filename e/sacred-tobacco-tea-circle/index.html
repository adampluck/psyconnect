🙏 The Call

A transformative evening : This evening is a bridge between worlds—between ancient and modern, inner and outer, Earth and Divine.

This is a chance to sit in sacred ceremony with an original wisdom keeper of the Amazon Rainforest.

This is more than an event, it is a sacred ceremony where ancestral knowledge, sacred medicines, and messages from the heart of the Amazon are shared with integrity and love,

Kurikindi holds a deep connection with the Divine energies & Great Spirit. It offers an opportunity for deep healing, helping us to grow spiritually and develop a deeper understanding of ourselves and the world around us.

Whether you are experienced or simply curious, we invite you to join our circle and share the traditional medicines of tobacco and chilli.

You are warmly welcome.

🌎 The Shaman - Kurikindi Golden Hummingbird

In a world that moves so quickly, where spiritual knowledge is often dispersed, we are fortunate to have shamans such as Kurikindi Golden Hummingbird, who brings teachings from the depths of the Amazon rainforest. He shares songs, spiritual teachings and ceremonies in the most traditional form.

Born and raised in the jungle in the simplest of ways, is part of the Achuar tribe.

Kurikindi began his training in shamanic knowledge and plant medicine at the age of five. His lineage stretches back thousands of years, unbroken on both his mother's and father's sides. Such a well-preserved lineage and body of wisdom are rare, even within the Amazon rainforest.

He has walked the path of shamanic knowledge in deep connection with Divine Wisdom, or the Great Spirit, dedicating his life to helping others with purity of intention.

Along side with his spiritual work, Kurikindi is a respected global advocate, speaking passionately on issues such as climate change, environmental justice, and Indigenous rights. Using his voice in internationally in spiritual assemblies around the world, as defender of the Amazon rainforest.

This evening, he will share tobacco and chilli - sacred plant medicines within his lineage - alongside the unaltered Divine wisdom & shamanic knowdlege passed down through his ancestors.

🌱 About the Sacred Medicines

Chilli Cleanse: Chilli medicine teaches us the art of centring, patience and presence in a deeply practical way. It's gently applied on the eye lids, and Its nature invites us to turn inward, quiet the mind and become fully present.

Traditionally used in preparation for spiritual work, chilli is associated with purification, humility and surrender. It reminds us to approach the learning journey with an open heart, innocent curiosity and a willingness to receive, helping us cultivate a deeper connection with ourselves and the spiritual world. (Please avoid wearing contact lenses.)

Sacred Tobacco (Tea): Traditionally regarded as a masculine plant, tobacco carries the energy of strength, grounding, clarity and direction. It invites us to align with our truth, find stability within ourselves and bring harmony to the masculine and feminine energies we hold within.

Tobacco is a teacher, supporting physical, emotional and spiritual resilience while encouraging self-discovery and inner balance.

In sacred ceremony, it is seen as a bridge to the spiritual world, helping us release negativity, reconnect with our inner wisdom and create space for clarity, healing and transformation.

Many people report feeling clearer, happier, more focused and deeply connected to themselves in the days following a ceremony.
