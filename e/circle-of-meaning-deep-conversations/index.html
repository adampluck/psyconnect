connection & intimacy

Circle of Meaning Conversations is a bi-weekly discussion group for people who want more than small talk. It’s a space to pause, reflect, and share meaningful dialogue in a world that often pulls us toward distraction and surface-level connection.

Every 2 weeks we gather in a supportive circle to explore one deep theme, from self-discovery and the masks we wear, to relationships, purpose, and how we navigate change. The format is simple but powerful: a short practice to ground ourselves after arriving, a guiding theme or question to focus our reflections, time in pairs or small groups for everyone to speak, and a whole-group dialogue where threads are woven together. We close with a short ritual or reflection so people leave feeling centred and connected.

Circle of Meaning is not therapy, and it’s not a lecture. It’s a community of equals, fellow travellers who value listening as much as speaking. The emphasis is on curiosity, presence, and respect. You don’t need prior knowledge of psychology, spirituality, or philosophy to take part; just an openness to engage in honest conversation and to hear others’ perspectives.

Why come? Many of us crave spaces where we can show up as ourselves, without judgement or pressure, and where conversations go beneath the surface. Circle of Meaning offers exactly that: a rhythm of weekly meetings where you can reflect, connect, and grow alongside others who are also asking life’s bigger questions.

Whether you are seeking clarity in your own journey, companionship in exploring the human experience, or simply a regular dose of meaningful conversation, this community is designed to hold and support you. Come as you are, and see what unfolds when we listen deeply and speak from the heart.
