Candlelit meditation experience in London. Mantra Lounge is a great alternative Friday night. Shake off the week, welcome in the weekend and wake up uplifted. The perfect place to go within & meet new friends.

We know that sound and music are powerful and have the ability to move us in incredible ways. Kirtan and Mantra meditation is a process of spiritual connection through sound. We allow our ears to take in the vibration of the Mantra and then use our voices to sing out the Mantra, filling the space with an unexpected symphony.

This enlivening and connecting form of meditation has its roots in an ancient spiritual culture, and has been practiced for thousands of years in India. We're stoked to have been able to share it in the heart of London since 2011.

As per our tradition, we finish off the evening with tea and vegan cake.
