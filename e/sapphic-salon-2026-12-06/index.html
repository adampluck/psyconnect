~ SAPPHIC SALON ~ An intentional space of sensuality & soul for the femmes & thems of London. Not a women’s circle, not a queer social mixer… but a mysterious third thing that will emerge and evolve as we each step in and say YES. It is an exclusively sapphic space: women (cis+trans) and nb sapphics of all ages, race, and experience level are invited and welcome. It is for the seasoned queers and the fresh baby gays. For any girl or woman or nb babe who wants to step OUT of heteronormativity and into more of who they came here to be. It is for those who feel the curiosity in their hearts, bodies, and souls to meet, and be met, in a deeper place. These events will be held monthly, on the first Sunday of each month. Each salon will have a different theme, and this will uniquely flavour the events from month to month, keeping them vibrant and alive. You can expect a nourishing combo of: - Gentle embodiment exercises - Self-enquiry prompts - Heart led relational practises - Creative expression - Subtle, consent-based erotic exploration - Ritual - Grounded integration Everything is an invitation, and you are always at choice - nothing is expected of you in this space. It is purely for what is honest, and real, and in devotion to the truth of each moment. Let your curiosity guide you. ~ SAPPHIC SALON ~ An intentional space of sensuality & soul for the femmes & thems of London.
