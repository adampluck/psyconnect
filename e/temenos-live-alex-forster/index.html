Live Improvised Music To Move The Body & Still The Mind 24 Oct 2026, 17:00 – 20:00 Old School Rooms, 2a Powerscroft Rd, Lower Clapton, London E5 0PU, UK TEMENOS LIVE... We are honoured and thrilled to welcome back the supremely talented Alex Forster to the Old School Rooms on Saturday 24th October to host an evening of live, improvised music to move the body and still the mind. Alex Forster needs little introduction. His music creations and legendary live performances range from meditative soundscapes to uplifting, lyrical rhythms. Drawing inspiration from the sights and sounds he has gathered on his global journeys as well as decades exploring movement practices on the dance floor, Alex weaves together musical journeys like no one else. As a multi-instrumentalist and master of live looping improvisation, Alex crafts bespoke soundscapes that invite participants to drop in, move freely, and be fully present. Whether performing in a forest, at a festival, or conscious dance space or workshop, his live sets are always transformative experiences. Alex will be doing what he does best - creating a one-of-a-kind, fully live musical journey, guided and inspired by the energy of the room and the dancers within it. Expect a deeply immersive, heart-opening evening of rhythm, sound, and movement. These 'Live Dances' with Alex are the most beautiful and powerful dances and always highlights of the Temenos calendar. Be sure to book early and join us for what is going to be another unmissable evening of movement. Tickets are available in three tiers on a first-come, first-served basis.
