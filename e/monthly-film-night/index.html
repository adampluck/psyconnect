Film night is a time to come together at the end of the week and digest a curated selection of thought provoking cinema. Each month we pick one film for its bigger themes of consciousness, myth, transformation and the human journey.

Enjoy the film inside our hi-fidelity sound treated studio with subs to really FEEL frequencies. There'll be time at the end to sit in groups and discuss 3 questions to spur more reflection and debate.

Practice Flow to Melt with Jordan beforehand then truly melt into the movie with drinks, popcorn. You're welcome to order takeaway to the studio too.
