Cacao Community is a time to sit, share, be witness and remember we're part of a wider community where we can learn and support each other on our life paths.

We use this container as a reminder to briefly step out of our individualistic bubbles and for a couple hours tap into the power of community, sharing and connection.

Something special happens when we witness each other in this way, a deep part of us relaxes and remembers we're not so alone in this life. We also honour and work with the spirit of the cacao plant to help open our hearts, be vulnerable and alchemist our awareness to move forward with intention in life.
