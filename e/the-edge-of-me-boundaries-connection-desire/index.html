The Edge of Me - Boundaries, Connection & Desire

Explore boundaries as thresholds for connection through somatic practice with Anna Noctuelle.

When
Thursday 22 October 2026 · 19:00
Where
Studio Ma, London E1 4BG
Host
Anna Noctuelle

connection & intimacyhealing & wellbeing

Where do you end and I begin? A boundary isn't necessarily a wall. It can be an edge, a threshold, a place where two people meet. We often think of boundaries as something we communicate with words: yes, no, stop, don't. But our bodies are communicating all the time — through tension, breath, movement, leaning in, pulling away, opening, closing, reaching or freezing. This workshop is an invitation to become more curious about those signals. Together, we'll explore how to find your boundaries, recognise them in the moment, and stay connected to yourself while connecting with someone else. We'll practise honouring what your body is telling you — and communicating it in ways that allow another person to meet you there. We'll explore: sensing the difference between yes, no, maybe, not yet, more and enough noticing the subtle bodily signals that appear before a boundary becomes a thought staying present with yourself when someone else wants something different expressing boundaries through words, movement, breath and touch holding a boundary without shutting down connection allowing someone closer without abandoning yourself discovering how a clear boundary can become an invitation rather than a rejection exploring the relationship between boundaries, vulnerability, trust and desire There will be guided somatic exercises, partnered exploration and space to experiment, play and reflect. You don't need to arrive knowing exactly where your boundaries are. In fact, that's part of the exploration. Because knowing your boundaries isn't about becoming harder to reach. It's about becoming clear enough to choose. When I know where I am, I can meet you more freely. When I can feel my no, my yes becomes clearer. And when I can communicate both, connection becomes much more interesting. Come curious. Come embodied. Find your edge — and discover what you want to do with it. All levels welcome About the educator: Anna is a somatic practitioner, Reiki practitioner and educator with a lifelong background in movement, somatics, trauma-informed practice and Tantra. Her work is driven by a desire to go beyond the surface — into t issue, movement, breath and sensation, and into what becomes possible when we slow down and listen to the body. With empathy, compassion and a deep sensitivity to the places where we lose connection with ourselves, she creates space for people to discover the knowledge their bodies already hold. Her work explores embodiment, intimacy, connection and desire, with a warm, curious and fully inclusive approach.
