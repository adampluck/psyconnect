Welcome to your monthly yogi book club, a space to come together as a community to learn the philosophy that underpins the practice.

Together the group will be studying The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali (Swami Vivekananda commentary but other copies are welcome if members already have them). All modern practices are in response to this: think of hatha, vinyasa, yin, whatever you do, as the vehicle and raja yoga (yoga of the mind, meditation, which this book explores) as the goal/destination. This is a key/crucial text all yoga practitioners should absorb themselves in, as they do, they will experience why.

The group will cover one chapter per month (there is so much wisdom in every page) so for September the focus is on Samadhi Pada, chapter 1. Members are asked to read, read again and note down their thoughts/reflections/experiences as they go. Come ready to share and discuss with a pen and notepad handy. Don't worry if studying or reading doesn't come naturally, or it's hard to understand, or conversely if it's already been studied before, everyone will learn together and from each other in a safe, supportive, loving environment and by the end life will have changed just a little bit, with a community to share that journey with too.
