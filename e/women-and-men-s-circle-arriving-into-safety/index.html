WOMEN'S AND MEN’S CIRCLE At The Threshold, Arriving Into Safety “Safety is not the absence of threat, it is a presence of connection.” -Gabor Maté This circle is not about the kind of safety that keeps us small, invisible and at home on the sofa. This is about the safety that allows us to step into the unknown, take risks, and be fully present and uncompromisingly ourselves. When we feel safe, there’s more agency, more potential, a sense of possibility and limitlessness, and more space for creativity and love. When we don't feel safe, we withdraw, we fawn, we people-please, we keep ourselves small, and we don't step outside our comfort zone. We keep things surface-level. At the same time, many of us quietly crave deeper connection and more from life. However, we don’t know where to find it, or how to create it. Because somewhere along the way, fear got better of us, and this state became our default. What we are going to explore: • Learn what safety means to you, and to us as a group • Understand what safety means to others, and how it may be experienced differently across genders • Learn how to recognise when safety is compromised and how to restore it • Use embodied practices to experience safety on a somatic level • Gain tools that enable you to regulate yourself and create a greater sense of safety within • Recognise and cultivate connection with another as one of the pathways to safety Real safety is about being able to access joy, play, connection and creativity. From this place, authentic relating becomes possible. In a way, safety is an illusion. There is no permanent state of safety, our nervous systems are constantly alert, impacted and overstimulated by today’s world. Our brains continually scan for danger. And yet, if safety is an illusion, how do we create enough inner stability to become agents of our own lives rather than victims of our circumstances? You’ll leave this workshop with a deeper understanding of how to access safety within your body, along with practical tools that enable you to do so. We'll explore the topic through embodied movement, spatial constellations, meditation, deep sharing and reflection. REFUNDS POLICY: Please note, we do not arrange refunds or exchanges.
