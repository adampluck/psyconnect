Good Company with John Prendergast

Join us in Person at Colet House or register below to receive the Zoom meeting link. Meeting schedule: 6.00–6.30: Kirtan — Margaret O’Keeffe (facilitating via Zoom) 6.30–7.00: Shared silent meditation 7.00–8.00: Voyage of Discovery — John Prendergast (facilitating in person) John Prendergast is a spiritual teacher, author, psychotherapist, and retired adjunct professor of psychology who offers residential and online retreats worldwide. As we open to the realization that personal identity is a social construct, we may discover a profound inner freedom that supports an ease of being and the natural unfolding of our humanity. We welcome all who wish to learn and share non-dual teachings to enhance inner peace, embrace fresh ideas and make new connections. Currently presented both on zoom and live at our home, Colet House, Barons Court, the evenings open with a simple practice such as kirtan, chair yoga or breathing exercises to prepare for a shared half hour’s silent meditation followed by a presentation. To register, just click the link below and you will receive an invitation to join. https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/tZYvc-qrrzIqHdaIYiCwcSyRDZGbLfOmkPgo Long term Study Society member, designer and artist Jonathan Leiserach invites presenters from various disciplines including philosophers, scientists, doctors, musicians, artists, writers, and spiritual teachers to share thoughts and experience on non-dual themes. Afterwards, for those who come to Colet House, there is the opportunity to join The Movements to Music classes which The Study Society has offered for over 70 years.
