T.W.C (Thursday Waves Community) is a weekly 5Rhythms class held by Jason Rowe and Community Teachers - Judit Juhasz and Flow Vulk - 8-10pm in the intimate and graceful space of St Luke's Church, London N7 9RE. Using the dynamic movement practice we will renew and find fresh ways of being with ourselves, with another and in a group. The Wave is a physical practice combining the different energetic rhythms of Flowing, Staccato, Chaos, Lyrical and Stillness. Class & Group guidelines are as follows: 1. We create sacred space together 2. ⁠We gather to practice the 5Rhythms 3. ⁠We refrain from talking on the dance floor 4. ⁠We turn our phones off 5. ⁠We are respectful of ourselves, of others and of the space Doors open at 7.45pm and close at 8.15pm Tube: Caledonian Road and Buses 17, 259, 393, 91 stop outside. Price: Sliding scale £20 Inclusion / £16 Standard / £13 Reduced / £11 Concessions. No prebooking and all payment accepted by card or cash on the door The main space has a heated sprung oak floor and great sound system. We suggest you bring a full water bottle, and top ups can be accessed via the kitchen if needed. We also recommend you wear dance clothes underneath where possible and bring an extra layer. Any physical contact inside the church is consensual. This class was founded in 2011 by Jason. 5Rhythms - Connection. Creativity. Community. For any further information please email twc5rhythms@hotmail.com https://www.5rhythms.com/classes/ThursdayWaves-329
