Meeting schedule: 6.00–6.30: Kirtan — Margaret O’Keeffe (facilitating via Zoom) 6.30–7.00: Shared silent meditation 7.00–8.00: Voyage of Discovery — Philip Marvin (facilitating in person) Philip Marvin has been associated with the Society as Chairman, Trustee and teacher for many years. He has been a teacher and student of non-dual philosophy for over half a century. We welcome all who wish to learn and share non-dual teachings to enhance inner peace, embrace fresh ideas and make new connections. Currently presented both on zoom and live at our home, Colet House, Barons Court, the evenings open with a simple practice such as kirtan, chair yoga or breathing exercises to prepare for a shared half hour’s silent meditation followed by a presentation.
