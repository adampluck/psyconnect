CULTURE REMIX: VOICE & SONG CIRCLE A fortnightly collective of voice and unity A space to come back to yourself and rise together through resonance in choral harmony. Through song and voice, this circle offers a simple way to release, connect, and land - no performance, no right way to do it, just presence and shared vibes. Alongside singing, breathwork and light exercises support expression and presence - helping everyone in the room find their own voice, harmonies, frequencies and depth of connection to self and the group. There will be a cultural mix of repertoires and styles to explore. The circle includes: ✦ Song and voice - open to all, no experience needed ✦ Presence and expression - light exercises to help you arrive and open up ✦ Conversation and wordplay - space to be heard and to listen ✦ Science and Soul - looking at frequencies, breath and depth of sound ✦ Rhythm and Roots - using key principles of heartbeat and grounding Why it matters: In a fast-moving, over-productive world, this is a space to put all of that down - to reconnect with your own voice and a sense of freedom, all in a way that asks nothing of you but your presence. Come as you are. Format: Fortnightly | Thursday evenings Venue: Colet House Jimi Lyons A graduate of the British Academy of New Music and recipient of its National Music Facilitator of the Year award, Jimi Lyons has spent over five decades as a respected musician and mentor at the heart of the UK music industry, co-founding the iconic Vibes & Pressure at Passing Clouds, Dalston with Natty. He's led drum and song circles worldwide in both Nyabinghi and other cultural, spiritual and musical traditions and worked alongside industry cornerstones such as Sade, Julian Marley, Danny Sims, Dennis Brown and Bunny Wailer, to name a few. Jimi brings that depth of experience into the circle with warmth and ease — holding space for voice and play, and opening the room to connection and presence. Trinity Goldie Transformation strategist for individuals and organisations, and conscious music facilitator and artist, Trinity Goldie has spent over three decades building community through sound, somatics and self-healing practice. Her foundations are rooted in kirtan and bhajans from her early quests, aged 19, to India and Tibet, however is always exploring new ways to connect to our heart and purpose. Trinity specialises in curating experiences, workshops and retreats that support individuals and groups in coming back to a space of wholesome resonance and remembering.
